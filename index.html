Hi, I’m Asilbek! I’m a master’s student in Computational Data Science at Khalifa University. I completed my B.Sc. in Software Engineering at New Uzbekistan University in 2025, where I later worked as a Research Assistant and Teaching Assistant.

My research explores reinforcement learning for energy-efficient railway systems. I’ve built simulation pipelines from real rail trajectory, gradient, and curvature data, and evaluated Q-learning variants and deep reinforcement learning models for train speed optimization.

I also taught Introduction to Programming at New Uzbekistan University, helping students with debugging and problem-solving. Previously, I interned at Huawei Uzbekistan, trained in Java web development at EPAM Systems, and attended the DGIST Summer School.

You can explore my research and publication below.
